# Hello. 

I am writing this post about the vibe-coding epidemic that is  coming faster than ever. More and more people are turning to get ai to generate their software for them. Most not understanding what the AI is doing. So this post today is not a warning but more of a way to escape the vibe coding cycle. First of all, vibe coding is essentially creating something that works. but ever stop to think where it gets its programming ability. It turns out it takes other peoples software and uses bits of it to create. 

# How can you escape? 

Well first you need to stop using the ai for coding. Use it as a tool to help you learn. That is what I do and it can be really helpful if you use it correctly. 

Second, go online. You will be surprised at how many tutorials you can find. You can use these to just help you get started understanding what everything does and how it works. 

Third and finally, Dont give up. Just keep trying and doing. The more you write, the more you begin to understand the core concepts and how it works. 

# Farewell 

Good bye now. I hope you take this message to heart and try not using ai to do everything.
